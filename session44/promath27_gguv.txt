Abstract
We analyze a possible minimal counterexample to the
Jacobian Conjecture P, Q with gcd(deg(P ), deg(Q)) = 16
and show that its existence depends only on the existence of
solutions for a certain Abel differential equation of the
second kind.
MSC(2010): 16S32, 16W20
Keywords: Jacobian, Abel differential equation.
