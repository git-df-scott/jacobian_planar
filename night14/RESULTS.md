COORDINATE are, as generated, of the shape `x + phi(y)`

    -3*y^2 + x + 4*y - 1 ;  x - y + 3 ;  x ;  x + 4*y - 1 ;  x - y + 2 ;

## Family notes recorded

* **F1** (random top form with 2+ distinct irreducible factors plus random
  lower terms): 0 / 20 U-passers.  The multi-place-at-infinity shape alone
  does not produce an empty critical locus when the lower terms are random.
* **F1b** (the same idea done constructively, P = c1*x + c2*x^(m+1)*y^n): the
  two-term member has empty critical locus by derivation; 9 / 20 passed here
  because half the generated members carry an added perturbation term, which
  can reintroduce a critical point (e.g. 2*x^4*y^3 + x^3*y^3 + x has a
  critical point at x = -1/2, y^3 = 4).
* **F2** (constant discriminant): 30 / 30 U-PASS by derivation; every member
  is, after a triangular change of variable, P = h0*v + c*(x-a)^n*v^2, whose
  fibre at 0 splits as v*(h0 + c*(x-a)^n*v).
* **F2b** (nonconstant discriminant D = h0^2 + d_n*x^n): 30 / 30 U-PASS by
  derivation; these are the objects that reach genus 2, and their split fibre
  sits at lambda = -d_n/(4c), derived from D + 4*g*lambda being a square.
* **F3** (near-coordinate twists): the family that produced SY COORDINATE
  verdicts -- 11 of its 18 U-passers -- as expected, since it is built from
  coordinates; the other 7 are of the u + u^m*w shape and are exactly the
  x + x^2*y pattern again.
* **F4** (random sparse with a linear term): 3 / 20 U-passers, all 3 with a
  reducible fibre.
