\section{The polynomial certificate}\label{sec:polynomial}

\subsection{Jacobian and collision}

Termwise differentiation gives
\[
 P_x=1,\qquad P_y=x^2,
\]
and
\[
 Q_x=x^4+x^6y^2,\qquad Q_y=1+x^6+x^8y^2.
\]
Hence
\[
JF=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&x^2\\
x^4+x^6y^2&1+x^6+x^8y^2
\end{pmatrix}
\]
and
\[
\det JF
=1+x^6+x^8y^2-x^2(x^4+x^6y^2)=1.
\]
The values at the three indicated \(\F_2\)-points are
\[
 F(0,1)=F(1,0)=F(1,1)=(0,1).
\]
They are distinct as \(k\)-points, so \(F\) is not injective on points.  A
coordinate-ring proof of noninvertibility is given in
Section~\ref{sec:noniso}.
