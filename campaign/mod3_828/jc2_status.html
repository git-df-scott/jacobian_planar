Plane Jacobian conjecture · status of the hunt

One shape, in one degree pair, is all that stands below 125.

A counterexample is a pair P, Q with J(P,Q)=1 whose degrees do not divide one another. Every such pair with max deg < 125 has been excluded except one — and that one splits into two Newton-polygon shapes, of which a single shape remains open.

The open shape

Proposition 4.3, and the edge the elimination found

The open case fixes both Newton polygons exactly and requires [P,Q] = x². Vertices must be nonzero. Reducing the resulting system isolated, unprompted, the seven interior lattice points of a single edge of Q — the slope-2 edge from (2,1) to (12,21), the line j = 2i − 3.

The trap

How this hunt nearly closed the case by accident

The residual contains one equation in only two variables:

−( d₃₄⁴ − 5·d₃₄²·d₄₇ + 7·d₄₇² ) / 3  =  0   discriminant = −d₃₄⁴ / 3  →  d₄₇ = d₃₄² (5 ± i√3) / 14

Over ℂ that always has two branches. Over GF(p) it has them only when −3 is a square, i.e. when p ≡ 1 (mod 3):

What this would have cost

A single run at p = 32003 propagates a forced zero that is false over ℂ, and the case “closes.” The bound moves from 108 to 125 — wrongly. Everything here is now done over ℚ, or at primes ≡ 1 (mod 3).

Where this goes next

Three moves, in order of odds

A
Solve the edge. It is one-dimensional and consistent — parametrize it, push the solution back up the substitution chain, and see whether the remaining 34 equations survive.
B
Finish the §5 redo. The reduction now has its own ODE, with the unique degree-14 solution f₁ = −y⁸(y+1)²(2048y⁴−512y³+320y²−240y+195)/6630. It stalls at collapsing 5 unknowns to one equation.
C
Case-split rather than eliminate. Gröbner blows up; branching on vanishing coefficients is how the original authors got through the sibling shape.
Standing rule

Any candidate pair passes an eight-gate check before it is called anything: characteristic zero, bracket verified two independent ways, non-dividing degrees, an interpolation proof of the identity, leading-form consistency, non-degeneracy, and multi-prime agreement. The gate was written before any candidate existed, and it rejects every known non-counterexample tested against it.
