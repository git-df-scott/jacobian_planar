# Task 3: published-exact face survivors

Only four of the 34 rows have published-exact reduced polygons in the sources
used by this audit. The other rows remain `UNCLEAR`; their conditional pattern
results are not promoted into this dossier.

## 1. `GGHV34-18`, degrees `(108,72)` — live `(72,108)` territory

For both Proposition 4.3 polygons the decisive weight is `(2,-1)`. Put

    u=x*y^2,
    P_face=x*p(u),       deg p=7,
    Q_face=x^2*y*q(u),   deg q=10.

Then

    [P_face,Q_face]=x^2*(p*q-3*u*p'*q+2*u*p*q'),

so the exact face equation is

    p*q-3*u*p'*q+2*u*p*q'=1.

After component scaling, the family has dimension one, generated by
`u -> t*u`. Normalizing `p(0)=q(0)=1` and the leading coefficient of `p` to
one leaves 16 unknowns and 16 equations. The residual group is `mu_7`; the
Hurwitz checker gives five covers and therefore 35 normalized coefficient
points. `face_families.json#/T9` gives every `q_j` recursively and the six
remaining exact equations in `p_1,...,p_6`.

Cascade level one is stored coefficient-by-coefficient in each chart's
`level1_cascade`: weight drop one, eight new P-slice coefficients, eleven new
Q-slice coefficients, and eighteen linear equations. The Hamiltonian direction
gives a kernel of dimension at least one.

## 2. `GGHV34-11`, degrees `(66,99)`, and `GGHV34-25`, `(72,108)`

Their published Proposition 4.2/4.1 charts share type T8. With

    u=x*y^3,
    P_face=x*y*p(u),     deg p=5,
    Q_face=x*q_raw(u),   deg q_raw=8,

and `q=-q_raw`, the normalized equation is

    p*q+3*u*p'*q-2*u*p*q'=1.

After component scaling the family again has dimension one. Fixing
`p(0)=q(0)=1` and leading(`p`)=1 leaves 12 unknowns and 12 equations, residual
group `mu_5`, two Hurwitz covers, and ten normalized coefficient points.
The explicit recurrence and four residual equations are
`face_families.json#/T8`.

Each first cascade level has six new P-slice coefficients, nine new Q-slice
coefficients, and fourteen coefficient equations, emitted in `triage.json`.
Both rows are eliminated beyond face level by GGHV 2022, Theorem 5.1, so they
are controls rather than live CE territory.

## 3. `GGHV34-08`, degrees `(56,84)`

Proposition 4.4 gives target `x` and polygons `2B,3B`, where

    B=conv{(0,0),(2,0),(3,1),(0,7)}.

No outer weight has top bracket weight equal to the target. Thus there is no
inhomogeneous essential-face equation: every outer face must merely commute,
with the standard form `P_edge=alpha*R_edge^2`,
`Q_edge=beta*R_edge^3`. The four outer weights and their first descending
coefficient systems are nevertheless emitted under
`commuting_outer_faces[*].level1_cascade` (5, 11, 14, and 33 equations,
depending on the edge). This row is already eliminated by GGHV 2022's
intersection argument and is ranked last.
