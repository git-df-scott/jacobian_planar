# Task 2: `(84,126)` coverage audit

## Verdict

**The coverage gap is not closed. The fourteen reduced charts are conditionally
empty, but the map from the published chain to those fourteen charts is not a
published theorem and fails as a universal chain-to-carrier rule.**

The concrete negative control is GGHV's F9 row. `trackD_chain_map.py` applies
its formula to `A0=(7,21), A1=(11/7,2)` and returns target `x^2`. GGHV 2022,
Proposition 4.4 instead performs the shears explicitly and obtains target `x`
and polygons generated by `2{(2,0),(3,1),(0,7)}` and
`3{(2,0),(3,1),(0,7)}`. `coverage_negative_control.py` checks this mismatch.

## What is actually proved for `(84,126)`

If a reduction produces any of the fourteen JSON charts, its essential face is
empty in characteristic zero: the fatal coefficients are `-26` and `39`, and
both are nonzero modulo 999983 and 1000003. This implication is exact.

What is not proved is the antecedent: that every realization of the published
chain `A0=(7,35), A1=(19/7,5)` reduces to one of those charts.

## Missing lemma

A case-specific reduction lemma is required. It must prove all of the following,
without assuming the desired edge exists:

1. identify the actual predecessor/lower corner after swapping coordinates;
2. derive the complete factorization and multiplicities of the relevant leading
   form using the hypotheses of the cited Corollary 7.4;
3. exhaust every shear root and every alternative predecessor supplied by the
   Proposition 3.12 starting-point analysis;
4. apply Proposition 8.2 only after checking its hypotheses, and determine the
   exceptional endpoint assignment rather than assuming both orientations;
5. determine the monomial twist exponent from the resulting polygon, hence the
   actual power `x^k` in the transformed bracket;
6. transform the full Newton polygon and prove that the top-left possibilities
   are exactly `c'=0,...,6`, with no missing or spurious charts.

GGHV 2022 carries out these steps separately for `(9,27)`, `(9,24)`, `(8,28)`,
and `(7,21)`; it does not state a parameterized lemma that can simply be
specialized to `(7,35)`. Consequently the campaign-safe statement is:

    FOURTEEN CONJECTURAL-PATTERN CHARTS: KILLED.
    PUBLISHED (84,126) ROW: UNCLEAR PENDING THE CASE-SPECIFIC REDUCTION LEMMA.
