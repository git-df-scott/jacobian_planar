# Queue coverage: is every enumerated admissible case in the above-125 queue?

Audit date 2026-08-20. Read-only audit; no existing file was modified, nothing was pushed,
no msolve/Singular was run.

**Question.** Prior work (`h2/w5_h2_target_provenance.py`, log `h2/target_provenance.log`)
checked *validity*: every target of `campaign/audit_tracks/trackD_targets.json` (180) corresponds
to a shape the independent enumeration produces. This audit checks the **covering** direction:
is every enumerated admissible case with 125 <= max <= 300 present in the queue, in some orientation?

**Answer: no — 444 of the 464 enumerated cases in [125,300] have no queue target.**
Twelve of those sit at max <= 150, inside [C] §6's *published, complete* 34-shape catalogue.
This is not (necessarily) an enumeration bug: the queue is by construction a *triage survivor*
list, not a sweep (see "Why the gap exists"). It had, however, never been measured.

## Matching relation

A queue target's `tag` is parsed exactly as `w5_h2_target_provenance.parse_tag` parses it, giving
`(A0, final corner, (m,n))`; family tags `F<k>(m,n)=..` and `F<k>(j=..;m,n=..)` are resolved through
`GGV_FAMILIES_L1/L2` to the family's fixed `(A0, A_1)`. An enumeration record carries
`A0`, `mid`, `final`, `m`, `n`, `deg_P`, `deg_Q`, `max`. Case and target are **equivalent** when

    (A0, final, {m,n})  agree,   with {m,n} an unordered pair (both orientations accepted).

This is the prior work's relation verbatim, and it ignores the *intermediate* corners `mid`,
because a chain tag's middle corners are not recoverable from a family tag. The ambiguity that
introduces is bounded and measured below — it does not affect any verdict here.

## Reverse direction (validity), re-derived here

Of the 180 targets, **179** match an enumerated case (any max <= 300) and **1** does not:

```
  F6(j=0;m,n=4,10) | a=5 b=5 c'=0 r=3 eps=((3, 1),(1, 0))   ->  A0=[5, 1, 20] final=[9, 5, 4] (m,n)=[4, 10] max=250
```

This is exactly the already-known discrepancy `gghv_audit/DISCREPANCIES.md` §D7: family F6's
growth law `m=3j+4, n=8j+10` yields `gcd(m,n)=2` at every even j, which [5] Definition 3.3's
coprimality clause excludes. Independent reproduction of a known finding, not a new one.

## Why the gap exists (what absence from the queue does and does not mean)

`trackD_targets.json` is not a sweep list. Per `campaign/audit_tracks/trackD_solve.py` and
`trackD_queue150.py`, a shape is appended only after clearing four gates:
compiler -> eps filter (`eps_P + eps_Q = (r+1,1)`) -> vertex probe -> y-adic sweep with
**dim bound <= 1**, plus a size cap. So a case can be missing from the queue because

* every one of its reduced shapes is vertex-dead (a genuine elimination), **or**
* its shapes survive the vertex probe but the sweep leaves dim >= 2 / the size cap rejects them
  — i.e. the case is *still open* and simply is not in the queue, **or**
* nothing above 150 that is not a family instance was ever generated at all
  (`trackD_generate.py` continues only the 24 families; [C] §6's 21 non-family rows would need
  Algorithms 6/8/9 to continue past 150 — the module says so itself).

For the twelve uncovered cases at max <= 150 the disposition can be read off
`campaign/audit_tracks/trackD_vertex.json`. All twelve were processed at the vertex stage;
**eight of them left vertex-LIVE shapes and were still not queued**, so their absence is a
triage decision (dim >= 2 or size), not an elimination:

## Matching ambiguity, measured

Because `mid` is not part of the key, 57 of the 362 in-range keys are shared by two or more
distinct chains (e.g. `(12,36)/12,33/11/3,8` and `(12,36)/9,24/11/3,8`, same A0, same final corner,
same (m,n)). Under those keys a target could be credited with covering a chain it does not name.
**None of the 19 queue keys is one of the ambiguous ones** (0 overlap), so every
covered/uncovered verdict in this report is independent of the choice to ignore `mid`.
Keys carrying two cases with identical `mid` are just the two orientations (m,n)/(n,m) and are
intentionally collapsed.
